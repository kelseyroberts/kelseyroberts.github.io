About Me

Welcome! My name is Kelsey Roberts and I'm currently a Data Scientist at Posit PBC (formerly RStudio) in Cincinnati, Ohio. I have a passion for creating interactive data applications using R and Shiny to make complex data accessible and engaging. With a background in sports analytics—having worked with the New York Knicks, Miami HEAT, and Indiana Pacers—I love finding creative ways to visualize and explore data. I hold an MS in Statistics and Analytics from the University of Arkansas. Outside of work, I'm an avid birder, which inspired these projects combining my technical skills with my love for ornithology.
